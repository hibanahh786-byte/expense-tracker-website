Privacy Policy & Data Security
1. Your Privacy is Absolute

At ExpenseTrack, we believe that your personal financial details belong solely to you. All user accounts are isolated at the database layer. No third-party data broker has access to your logged expenses.

2. Secure Password Encryption

Your password is encrypted using the industry-standard bcrypt hashing algorithm with 10 salt rounds before being stored. Even database administrators cannot view your raw password.

3. Stateless JWT Authentication

We utilize cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored securely within your browser's localStorage. Tokens expire automatically and ensure zero session interception.

4. Public Landing Page Policy

This public landing page displays only synthetic sample preview data. Actual private financial records are accessible only after successful user authentication.
